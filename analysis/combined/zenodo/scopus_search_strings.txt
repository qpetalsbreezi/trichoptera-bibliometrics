Scopus search strings used for this study
========================================================================
Retrieval date: 12 April 2026.

API endpoint: https://api.elsevier.com/content/search/scopus
Field scope: TITLE-ABS-KEY (title, abstract, and author keywords).
Date limits: fetched year-by-year with PUBYEAR = YYYY for YYYY = 2010..2025.
  (Large years were split by month/quarter when needed for the 5,000-hit API cap.)
Document-type filter: NOT applied in the Scopus query string.
  Article and Review records were retained downstream in analysis
  (scripts/lib/pipeline.py :: filter_article_review / ANALYSIS_DOCUMENT_TYPES).
Language filter: NOT applied at retrieval (no LANGUAGE() clause).
Source of query strings: config/queries.json

------------------------------------------------------------------------
Group: Culicidae
query_id: mosquitoes

Executable query template (as sent to Scopus Search API):
  TITLE-ABS-KEY("mosquito" OR "mosquitoes" OR "Culicidae") AND (PUBYEAR = YYYY)
  with YYYY replaced by each integer year from 2010 through 2025.

Base TITLE-ABS-KEY clause:
  TITLE-ABS-KEY("mosquito" OR "mosquitoes" OR "Culicidae")

Example for 2020:
  TITLE-ABS-KEY("mosquito" OR "mosquitoes" OR "Culicidae") AND (PUBYEAR = 2020)
